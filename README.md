# Transfer Flow Utility

Transfer Flow Utility is a planned human-in-the-loop tool that helps members of a software development team align on ideas and transfer work clearly and efficiently.

## Problem

Development teams commonly face two collaboration problems:

- **Idea alignment:** Team members may have different interpretations of the same topic or decision and may not reach a shared understanding.
- **Work handoff:** Important context, completed work, current status, and next steps may be unclear when work moves from one team member to another.

## Approach

The tool will:

1. Condense team discussions, decisions, and handoff information with AI.
2. Present the final decision, its reason, completed work, current status, and next steps clearly.
3. Ask a human reviewer to correct and approve the summary.
4. Give the reviewed summary to the team for idea alignment and work handoff.

## Human-AI Boundary

AI produces a draft summary. Humans make the final decisions, verify technical accuracy, and decide whether the summary can be used.
