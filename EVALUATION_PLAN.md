# Draft Evaluation Plan

## Problem Grounding

### Who specifically has the collaboration problem we are addressing?

- Members of multi-person software development teams who need to align on ideas and decisions or hand work from one person to another.

### What do they currently do instead of our tool?

- Team members discuss the same idea or decision through meetings, messages, and documents, but may still have different interpretations and fail to reach a shared understanding.
- They hand work over through verbal explanations or documents of inconsistent quality, where important context, current status, and next steps may be unclear or implicit.

### What would be observably different if our tool worked?

- Team members would be more likely to describe the same final decision and its reason consistently.
- The receiving team member would need less time and fewer clarification questions to understand the completed work, current status, and next steps.

## Evaluation Plan Draft

### Success Definition

- A human reviewer can verify the technical accuracy of an AI-summarized document.
- Team members can correctly identify the final decision and its reason after reading the condensed document.
- A receiving team member can correctly identify the completed work, current status, pending work, and next action from the handoff summary.
- The AI summarizes ideas, decisions, and handoff information. Humans make the final decisions and approve the summary before the team uses it.

### Target Users

- Two to three software engineering students or developers who have similar team-collaboration experience.

### Method

- We will use structured observation and pre- and post-test interviews.
- Participants will complete two comparable collaboration scenarios involving a team decision and a work handoff.
- We will show them the original information and documents generated by our tool, then conduct a quiz about the final decision, its reason, work status, and next action.
- We will record completion time, quiz scores, and the number of clarification questions.

### Minimum Evidence Threshold

- Average completion time decreases by at least 20 percent.
- Average test scores improve by at least 20 percentage points.
- At least two-thirds of users correctly identify the same final decision, its reason, the current work status, and the next action.
- At least two-thirds of users provide positive feedback.
- A human reviewer finds no critical technical inaccuracies in the AI-summarized document.
